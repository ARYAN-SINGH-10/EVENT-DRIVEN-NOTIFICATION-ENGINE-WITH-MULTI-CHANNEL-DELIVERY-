## Entities & Tables

### 1. `NotificationEvent`
Stores incoming raw events before processing. Used for idempotency, replayability, and ingestion tracking.
- **Important Indexes:** `idx_event_user_type (userId, eventType)`, `idx_event_correlation (correlationId)`

### 2. `NotificationEntity`
The core tracked notification dispatched to the user. Tracks delivery attempts, status states (`CREATED`, `ROUTED`, `FAILED`, `DLQ`, etc.), and metadata.
- **Important Indexes:** `idx_notification_user (userId)`, `idx_notification_status (status)`

### 3. `NotificationAudit`
Immutable ledger of all state machine transitions for compliance and tracing.
- **Important Indexes:** `idx_audit_notification (notificationId)`

### 4. `NotificationPreference`
User-level configuration managing global vs channel-level opt-ins and quiet hours.
- **Important Indexes:** `idx_pref_user_event (userId, eventType)`

### 5. `NotificationTemplate`
Stores Velocity/Freemarker-style templates for channels.
- **Important Indexes:** `idx_template_name (name, channel, locale)`

### 6. `NotificationAnalytics`
Aggregated daily metrics generated by the `AnalyticsRollupScheduler`. Contains delivery rates and cost structures.
- **Important Indexes:** `idx_analytics_date_channel (date, channel)`
